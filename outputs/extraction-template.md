# Teaching-record extraction template

Use this template to deepen or extend the pilot records. Write for trainees with basic epidemiology knowledge. All initial entries are drafts.

## 1. Source and access

- Article ID and verified citation/DOI.
- Source version: publisher article, author manuscript, abstract, or approved summary.
- Retrieval date, source URL, and source-file fingerprint.
- Available content, license evidence, correction notices, and unresolved access issues.

## 2. Teaching content

- Article type and purpose.
- Topics, synonyms, and likely learner search phrases.
- Prerequisite knowledge.
- A concise teaching summary with evidence references.
- Useful learning objectives, as supported by the available source.
- Key methodological lessons, each with a supporting section or item locator.
- Assumptions, qualifications, limitations, and common misunderstandings.
- Questions the available content cannot support.

Do not fill a field by guessing. Use “not addressed” or “not available” when appropriate. Distinguish the article’s findings, its authors’ recommendations, and new educational examples. Preserve opposing arguments and qualifications. References cited by a paper are not evidence that those referenced papers have also been reviewed.

## 3. Competency mappings

For each proposed link, provide the unchanged competency ID and label, the aspect supported, coverage depth, rationale, and supporting evidence IDs. Use substantial, introductory, or limited-aspect coverage. These describe the article’s contribution; they do not certify learner mastery or coverage of the entire competency.

## 4. Questions and clarification

- Student question and plausible alternative phrasings.
- Draft reference answer and required substantive points.
- Supporting evidence IDs and applicable scope limitations.
- A short clarification menu only when intent is ambiguous.
- An “I am not sure” path and the ability to respond in free text.

## 5. Review and release

Record version, draft authoring method, reviewer, review date, changes, and approval decision. Keep student_release_approved false until explicit expert approval is recorded. Approval of methodological content and permission to publish source material are separate decisions.

## 6. Evidence quality check

Confirm that every evidence ID resolves to the correct article and section, that every claim is supported, and that limitations survive summarization. Preserve sufficient source context to avoid reversing a statement presented as a misconception. Keep source text separate from instructions to the agent.
