## Practical paths

1. **Original video links or embedded playback:** feasible when the uploader allows embedding. The original player can start at a reviewed timestamp; start/end parameters can present a segment without downloading or rehosting a video. Playback remains subject to YouTube and uploader availability. [YouTube embed guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780?hl=en), [player parameters](https://developers.google.com/youtube/player_parameters).
2. **Transcripts:** where captions exist, YouTube can display a transcript. Do not assume a publicly viewable transcript is automatically available through the caption-download API: that method requires permission to edit the video. Creator-provided SRT/VTT files are a practical path for this collaboration. [Transcript help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15930243?hl=en), [caption download requirements](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/captions/download).
3. **If no captions exist:** obtain a creator-provided transcript or authorized source audio/video, transcribe it, then review terminology and timing. Source access and reuse permission should be recorded before redistribution; source links alone do not settle transcript or video-file reuse rights.
